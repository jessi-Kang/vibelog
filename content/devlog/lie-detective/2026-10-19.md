## What I did

I made a 12-second vertical intro video for the game's first screen. The two leads, Jihoo and Haewon, pass a photo across a table and check it. I added a separately made music track and the game logo. I also made a draft title screen using the same logo.

## Why

When someone first opens the game on their phone, I wanted to show what these two do and how they work together, without any dialogue. My first intro was 26 seconds long. It stitched together six short AI-generated motion clips. But every scene felt frozen. Transition effects and small camera moves didn't fix that. So I rebuilt the scene around a single action, handing over a photo and checking it, and made it again as a 12-second video.

## Rabbit holes

At first I asked the video-generating AI to keep both people in frame the whole time. When the camera moved, one of them got pushed off screen. So I changed it so the camera starts on Jihoo, follows the hand and the photo being passed, and ends on Haewon. Next I had the photo lifted and turned slightly, but the front and back of the photo swapped and it became unclear where the hand was holding it. In the end I simplified it: the photo slides across the table instead of being lifted, and the other person checks it with their eyes.

## Next up

I'll keep watching the 12-second video and tune how the motion and music fit together.
