## 뭘 했나
새 프로젝트의 첫 글이 소개 글로 나오게 했습니다. 이 블로그는 제가 AI와 같이 만드는 서비스들의 [[데브로그|만드는 과정을 적은 개발 일지]]를 매일 밤 AI가 써서 저절로 올립니다. 이제 새 프로젝트가 들어오면 AI가 그 [[레포|코드와 문서를 모아 두는 저장소]]의 기획서와 코드를 읽고, 무엇을 누구를 위해 만들려는지 짧게 소개하는 글을 먼저 씁니다. 이때 프로젝트를 한두 문장으로 줄인 설명도 같이 씁니다. 그다음 글들은 제목 아래에 이 설명이 붙고, "lie-detective" 같은 레포 이름 대신 "거짓말 탐정" 같은 서비스 이름이 나옵니다. 이 밖에 글 아래 댓글과 용어 설명 말풍선도 붙였습니다.

## 왜

## What I did
New projects now open with an introduction post. This blog publishes a [[devlog|a development diary of how something is built]] every night, written by AI, for the services I build with AI. Now when a new project comes in, the AI reads the planning doc and code in its [[repo|the storage for a project's code and documents]] and first writes a short post about what it's meant to be and who it's for. At the same time, it writes a one- or two-sentence summary of the project. Every later post shows that summary under its title, and uses the service name, like "Lie Detective," instead of the repo name, like "lie-detective." Besides this, I added comments under posts and tap-to-open explanations for technical terms.

## Why
Until now, a project's first post started with "what I did that day." Someone arriving for the first time had to read without knowing what the project was. The first post for a project called Anchor never said what Anchor was. A one-line repo description wasn't enough.

## Rabbit holes
During the day I tried writing 5 introduction posts for my existing projects, and they were too detailed. They listed screens and features. Lie Detective is a service where people have to guess the right answers, and its post said which file held part of the answer rules. That was as good as showing how to find the answers. The cause was the writing instructions I had given the AI. They assumed it was introducing a project that was already well along. But an introduction post comes out on the first night after a project is registered, when almost nothing has been built yet. So I took the 5 posts down and changed the instructions: the AI now looks at the repo as it was around registration, not today's code, and only says "this is what I'm setting out to build." Answers and anything that shouldn't be public stay out, even if the code shows them.
