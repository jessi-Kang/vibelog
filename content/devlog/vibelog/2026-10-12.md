## What I did

I fixed the nightly writing step that skipped most of the day's work. Now it reads every commit from the day. I also made it possible to rewrite a post that's already up, using all of that day's [[commits|saved checkpoints of work, one chunk at a time]]. Beyond that, I tweaked the pictures, scene transitions, and pauses between sentences in the shorts that go out with each post.

## Why

Yesterday's post said nothing about the comments feature I built that day. The biggest change of the day was missing from the post entirely.

## Rabbit holes

The post had no mention of comments. It turned out it was only fetching the latest 20 commits. Of the 121 commits that day, 101 never made it into the post. On top of that, commits it has already passed are not read again the next night. Left alone, that story would never show up. So I made it fetch every commit, and added a way to rewrite a past day's post separately.
