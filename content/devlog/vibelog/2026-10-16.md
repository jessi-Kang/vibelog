## 뭘 했나

글 맨 아래에 바로 앞뒤 데브로그를 한 편씩 붙였습니다. [[데브로그|개발 과정을 적어 둔 글]]는 홈에 보이는 순서대로, 다른 프로젝트 글까지 섞여서 이어집니다. 이 밖에 폰에서 글 제목이 일찍 줄바꿈되던 것과 [[쇼츠|1분 안쪽의 세로 영상]] 속 글자도 손봤습니다.

## 왜

홈에서 최근 글을 보고 들어와 끝까지 읽으면, 아래에는 같은 프로젝트의 다른 날 링크 하나뿐이었습니다. 다른 최근 글을 보려면 홈으로 돌아가야 했습니다.

## 삽질 포인트

처음에는 본문 바로 아래에 최근 글 3편을 붙였습니다. 그랬더니 글을 다 읽기도 전에 다른 글 목록이 끼어들었습니다. 사람들은 글을 다 읽고 댓글까지 내려온 다음에야 다른 글을 찾습니다. 그래서 목록을 댓글 아래로 옮겼습니다.

3편도 많았습니다. 글 하나를 다 읽고 나면 고를 것이 많아서 오히려 넘어가기 어려웠습니다. 그래서 바로 앞 한 편과 바로 뒤 한 편만 남겼습니다.

## 다음 할 것

글 끝에서 다음 글로 넘어가는 사람이 늘었는지 지켜보겠습니다.

## What I did

I added the previous and next [[devlog|a post about how something is being built]] at the bottom of each post, one of each. They follow the same order as the home page, so posts from other projects come up too. I also fixed post titles that wrapped too early on phones, and touched up the text in the [[shorts|vertical videos under a minute]].

## Why

If you came in from a recent post on the home page and read to the end, the only thing below was a link to another day of the same project. To read another recent post, you had to go back home.

## Rabbit holes

At first I put three recent posts right under the body. But that list showed up before you had finished reading. People look for another post only after they finish reading and scroll past the comments. So I moved the list below the comments.

Three was also too many. After finishing a post, having that many to choose from made it harder to move on. So I kept only the one right before and the one right after.

## Next up

I'll watch whether more people go from the end of a post to the next one.

## Other news

**The weekly [[commit|a saved chunk of work]] count on the home page no longer includes bot commits.** The count showed 584, mixed with many commits from a [[bot|a program that runs automatically instead of a person]] that commits on its own every 30 minutes, so now it counts only commits people made. _1 commit_

**Summaries in the post list no longer repeat the title.** In 24 of 43 posts, the summary was nearly the same sentence as the title; now the AI writes the summary separately when it writes the post, and it says something the title doesn't, like why I did it or what came of it. _1 commit_

**You can see the commit rules on a page inside the site.** Posts on this blog are written by AI reading the day's commits, so how the commits are written decides how good the posts are. _3 commits_
